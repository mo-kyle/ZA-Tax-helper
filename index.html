Where your tax actually comes from

Each rung is taxed at its own rate, and only the income sitting inside it. Moving up a bracket re-rates the slice above that rung’s floor — never everything you earn.

    This is an estimate, not an assessment. It covers the ordinary salaried case: salary, a bonus, retirement contributions, medical credits, section 18A donations and PAYE already paid. It does not handle travel allowances or company cars, share incentives, lump sums from a fund, capital gains, foreign income or the section 10(1)(o)(ii) exemption, or the interest and dividend exemptions — enter other income already net of those. Retirement contributions above the annual limit are shown as disallowed; SARS carries them forward to a later year, which this does not model.

    Rates are the published SARS tables in tax-tables.js, covering the 2025, 2026 and 2027 years of assessment. Check them against sars.gov.za before relying on a number, and see your tax practitioner for anything that matters.
